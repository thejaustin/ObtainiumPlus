# Translation credits

Obtainium+ is translated by people, and this page exists to thank them. Generated by `scripts/dev/translation-provenance.py`; do not edit by hand.

**13895 strings** were written by human translators. 212 more are AI-written drafts that no person has reviewed yet; they are listed separately, credited to no person, and are replaced as soon as a human translation is approved. 47645 entries are still English.
